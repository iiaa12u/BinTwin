# Review Materials

This folder contains supporting materials for the structured literature synthesis conducted as part of the BinTwin study.

## Available Materials

- **Table S7 – Studies Included in the Final Qualitative Synthesis:** List of the 41 studies included in the final synthesis.
- **Table S8 – Search Strategy and Applied Filters:** Search queries, search fields, and filters applied across IEEE Xplore, Scopus, and the ACM Digital Library.
- **Table S9 – Full-Text Exclusion Log:** Study-level log of reports excluded during full-text assessment and their primary reasons for exclusion.

These materials are provided to improve the transparency and reproducibility of the review process.
